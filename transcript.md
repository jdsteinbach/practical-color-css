### Understanding the "from" Syntax

At the wrapper level, we need a CSS color function. You may have called these color notations or color syntax: `rgb()`, `hsl()`, etc. Technically, they're functions. They take a bunch of arguments going in and return a color. Ergo, the thing that follows the word `from` has to be a color. This could be a color. It could be a keyword. It could be a hex notation. It could be an `rgb()` function. It could even be a variable that contains a color. It could be a function that returns a color (think about `light-dark()`). And then `from` takes the color and turns its channel data into locally-scoped named variables for you. And those are right there.
